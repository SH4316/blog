### Keycloak 사용 분석

```
https://sandori.kr/auth/realms/Sandori/protocol/openid-connect/auth
```

- redirect_uri=`https%3A%2F%2Fsandori.kr%2Fmeal-web%2Fauth%2Fcallback&response_type=code`
- scope=`openid+profile+email`
- state=
- nonce=
- code_challenge=
- code_challenge_method=`S256`

`/meal-web`에서 로그인 을 누르면 `/auth/realms/Sandori/` 로 넘어간다.
